1. Covalent Particles, MO Theory & Complexes (L23)

Electronegativity (Pauling & Mulliken, with $E$ in $\text{eV}$) & Dipoles:

$$|\chi_A - \chi_B|_{\text{Pauling}} = \sqrt{E_{\text{diss}}(AB) - \frac{1}{2}\left[E_{\text{diss}}(AA) + E_{\text{diss}}(BB)\right]}$$ $$\chi_{\text{Mulliken}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(E_{\text{ion}} + E_{\text{aff}}\right), \qquad \mu = Q \cdot r, \qquad b = \frac{1}{2}(N - N^*)$$

Frost–Musulin Formula (Hückel Cyclic $\pi$-Systems) & Ligand Field Theory:

$$E_m = \sin\left(m \cdot \frac{360^\circ}{n} - 90^\circ\right) \cdot 2\beta \qquad (m = 0, 1, \dots, n-1)$$ $$\Delta_O\text{ (mol)} = \frac{N_A \cdot h \cdot c}{\lambda}, \qquad \mu_{\text{spin-only}} = \mu_B\sqrt{n(n+2)}$$

2. Matter and Radiation: Vibrational & UV/Vis Spectroscopy (L25)

Photodissociation Threshold & Quantum Harmonic Oscillator:

$$\lambda_{\text{diss}} = \frac{N_A \cdot h \cdot c}{E_{\text{diss}}(\text{mol})}, \qquad m_{\text{eff}} = \frac{m_1 \cdot m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$$ $$\tilde{\nu} = \frac{1}{2\pi \cdot c}\sqrt{\frac{k_f}{m_{\text{eff}}}}, \qquad \tilde{G}(v) = \frac{E_v}{h \cdot c} = \left(v + \frac{1}{2}\right)\tilde{\nu}$$

Lambert–Beer Law of Spectrophotometry:

$$A = -\log_{10}(T) = -\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right) = \varepsilon \cdot c \cdot L \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad T = 10^{-A}$$
Fundamental Constants (Lecture Reference):
  • Planck constant: $h = 6.6261 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J s}$; Speed of light: $c = 2.9979 \times 10^8\text{ m s}^{-1}$ ($2.9979 \times 10^{10}\text{ cm s}^{-1}$)
  • Avogadro constant: $N_A = 6.0221 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$; Elementary charge: $e = 1.6022 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$
  • Atomic mass unit: $1\text{ u} = 1.66054 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$; Debye: $1\text{ D} = 3.33564 \times 10^{-30}\text{ C m}$
⚠️ Golden Unit Conversion Rules (Bonding & Spectroscopy):
  • Pauling & Mulliken Formulas: All ionization, affinity, and bond dissociation energies MUST be plugged in in $\text{eV}$ ($1\text{ eV} \widehat{=} 96.485\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$)!
  • IR Wavenumber $\tilde{\nu}$ in $\text{cm}^{-1}$: Use $c = 2.9979 \times 10^{10}\text{ cm s}^{-1}$ in $\tilde{\nu} = \frac{1}{2\pi c}\sqrt{k_f / m_{\text{eff}}}$ with $k_f$ in $\text{N m}^{-1}$ ($\text{kg s}^{-2}$) and $m_{\text{eff}}$ in $\text{kg}$!
  • Lambert–Beer Doubling Rule: Doubling path length $L$ or concentration $c$ doubles absorbance $A$, which squares the fractional transmittance ($T_{\text{new}} = T_{\text{old}}^2$)!

Guided Example 1 • Ref: L23_Covalent Particles.pdf (Ex. 11, 12 & 13) & Q02_Quizzes_Lecture2.pdf (2nd Rep Q2 & Q3)

Pauling vs. Mulliken Electronegativity ($\text{HCl}$), Heteronuclear MO Bond Order ($\text{CO}$) & Hückel Benzene ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$)

Problem Statement:
(a) Electronegativity (`L23` Ex. 11b): Given the bond dissociation energies $E_{\text{diss}}(\text{HCl}) = 4.44\text{ eV}$, $E_{\text{diss}}(\text{H}_2) = 4.48\text{ eV}$, and $E_{\text{diss}}(\text{Cl}_2) = 2.61\text{ eV}$, as well as ionization energies and electron affinities $E_{\text{ion}}(\text{Cl}) = 12.3\text{ eV}$, $E_{\text{aff}}(\text{Cl}) = 3.61\text{ eV}$, $E_{\text{ion}}(\text{H}) = 13.6\text{ eV}$, $E_{\text{aff}}(\text{H}) = 0.75\text{ eV}$, calculate the electronegativity difference $\Delta\chi$ between $\text{H}$ and $\text{Cl}$ according to both Pauling and Mulliken.
(b) Molecular Orbital Theory (`L23` Ex. 12 & `Q02` 2nd Rep Q2): Determine the valence MO electron configuration and bond order $b = \frac{1}{2}(N - N^*)$ of carbon monoxide ($\text{CO}$, $10$ valence electrons) and compare it to $\text{O}_2^+$ ($11$ valence electrons) and $\text{O}_2^{2-}$ ($14$ valence electrons).
(c) Frost–Musulin Hückel Theory (`L23` Slide 46 & `Q02` 2nd Rep Q3): Calculate the relative $\pi$-MO energies $E_m = \sin\left(m\frac{360^\circ}{n} - 90^\circ\right)\cdot 2\beta$ for benzene ($n=6$), the total $\pi$-electron energy $E_{\pi,\text{tot}}$, and the HOMO–LUMO gap in units of $\beta$.

🎙️ Lecturer Board Moderation (Guided Example 1):

"Point out why Pauling's formula takes the difference between the actual heteronuclear bond energy $E_{\text{diss}}(AB)$ and the arithmetic mean of the homonuclear bonds $\frac{1}{2}[E_{\text{diss}}(AA)+E_{\text{diss}}(BB)]$: a purely covalent bond would equal the average of $A\text{–}A$ and $B\text{–}B$, whereas ionic resonance stabilization makes $A\text{–}B$ stronger!"

"In Step (c), remind students to set their calculator to DEGREES (`DEG`) before using the Frost–Musulin formula $E_m = \sin(m \cdot 360^\circ/n - 90^\circ) \cdot 2\beta$. For $m=0$, $\sin(-90^\circ) = -1$, so the lowest orbital in ANY Hückel ring always has energy $E_0 = -2\beta$!"

Step (a) – Pauling vs. Mulliken Electronegativity Difference for $\text{HCl}$:
$$\Delta\chi_{\text{Pauling}} = \sqrt{E_{\text{diss}}(\text{HCl}) - \frac{1}{2}\left[E_{\text{diss}}(\text{H}_2) + E_{\text{diss}}(\text{Cl}_2)\right]} = \sqrt{4.44 - 0.5(4.48 + 2.61)} = \sqrt{0.895} = \mathbf{0.95}$$ $$\chi_{\text{Cl,Mulliken}} = \frac{1}{2}(12.3 + 3.61) = \mathbf{7.96\text{ eV}}, \qquad \chi_{\text{H,Mulliken}} = \frac{1}{2}(13.6 + 0.75) = \mathbf{7.18\text{ eV}}$$ $$\Delta\chi_{\text{Mulliken}} = 7.96 - 7.18 = \mathbf{0.78\text{ eV}}$$
Step (b) – MO Bond Order $b = \frac{1}{2}(N - N^*)$:

For $\text{CO}$ ($4 + 6 = 10$ valence electrons): $(1\sigma)^2 (2\sigma^*)^2 (1\pi_x)^2 (1\pi_y)^2 (3\sigma)^2 \implies N = 8$ bonding, $N^* = 2$ antibonding:

$$b(\text{CO}) = \frac{1}{2}(8 - 2) = \mathbf{3.0} \quad (\text{triple bond})$$

For oxygen species (`L23` Ex. 6): $b(\text{O}_2^+) = \frac{1}{2}(8-3) = \mathbf{2.5} > b(\text{O}_2) = \mathbf{2.0} > b(\text{O}_2^-) = \mathbf{1.5} > b(\text{O}_2^{2-}) = \mathbf{1.0}$ (higher bond order $\implies$ shorter bond length!).

Step (c) – Frost–Musulin Energies of Benzene ($n = 6$, $6$ $\pi$-Electrons):
$$E_0 = \sin(-90^\circ)\cdot 2\beta = \mathbf{-2\beta}, \quad E_1 = E_5 = \sin(\pm 30^\circ)\cdot 2\beta = \mathbf{-\beta}, \quad E_2 = E_4 = \mathbf{+\beta}, \quad E_3 = \mathbf{+2\beta}$$ $$E_{\pi,\text{tot}} = 2(-2\beta) + 4(-\beta) = \mathbf{-8\beta}, \qquad \Delta E_{\text{HOMO}\to\text{LUMO}} = E_2 - E_1 = (+\beta) - (-\beta) = \mathbf{2\beta}$$
Guided Example 2 • Ref: L25_Matter and Radiation.pdf (Exercises 1, 7, 8 & 10)

Photodissociation of $\text{Cl}_2$ (Ex. 1), Harmonic Oscillator of $^1\text{H}^{35}\text{Cl}$ (Ex. 10) & Lambert–Beer Law (Ex. 7)

Problem Statement:
(a) Photodissociation (`L25` Ex. 1): The molar bond dissociation energy required to cleave $\text{Cl}_2$ molecules into $\text{Cl}$ radicals is $E_{\text{diss}}(\text{mol}) = 239\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$. Calculate the threshold wavelength $\lambda_{\text{diss}}$ (in $\text{nm}$).
(b) Vibrational Spectroscopy (`L25` Slide 80 & Ex. 10): The $^{1}\text{H}^{35}\text{Cl}$ molecule ($m_1 = 1.008\text{ u}$, $m_2 = 34.97\text{ u}$, $1\text{ u} = 1.66054 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}$) has a harmonic force constant of $k_f = 516.0\text{ N m}^{-1}$. Calculate its reduced mass $m_{\text{eff}}$ (in $\text{kg}$) and fundamental vibrational wavenumber $\tilde{\nu}$ (in $\text{cm}^{-1}$).
(c) UV Spectrophotometry (`L25` Ex. 7): A $c = 0.0100\text{ mol L}^{-1}$ solution of benzene in a cuvette of path length $L_1 = 0.200\text{ cm}$ transmits $T_1 = 48.0\%$ of incident UV light at $256\text{ nm}$. Calculate the absorbance $A_1$, the molar absorption coefficient $\varepsilon$ (in $\text{L mol}^{-1}\text{ cm}^{-1}$), and the new percentage transmittance $T_2$ when the path length is doubled to $L_2 = 0.400\text{ cm}$.

🎙️ Lecturer Board Moderation (Guided Example 2):

"In Step (b), highlight why reduced mass $m_{\text{eff}} = \frac{m_{\text{H}} m_{\text{Cl}}}{m_{\text{H}} + m_{\text{Cl}}} \approx 0.980\text{ u}$ is almost equal to the mass of the light hydrogen atom alone: when a heavy chlorine atom is attached to a light hydrogen atom, the hydrogen atom does almost all the vibrating!"

"In Step (c), warn students against the linear intuition trap: if $0.20\text{ cm}$ absorbs $52\%$ of the light ($T_1 = 48\%$), $0.40\text{ cm}$ does NOT absorb $104\%$! Absorbance $A = -\log_{10} T$ doubles ($0.319 \rightarrow 0.638$), so transmittance is squared: $T_2 = (0.48)^2 = 0.230 = 23.0\%$."

Step (a) – Photodissociation Threshold Wavelength of $\text{Cl}_2$:
$$\lambda_{\text{diss}} = \frac{N_A \cdot h \cdot c}{E_{\text{diss}}(\text{mol})} = \frac{6.0221 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1} \cdot 6.6261 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J s} \cdot 2.9979 \times 10^8\text{ m s}^{-1}}{2.39 \times 10^5\text{ J mol}^{-1}} = 5.005 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m} = \mathbf{500\text{ nm}}$$
Step (b) – Reduced Mass $m_{\text{eff}}$ & Harmonic Wavenumber $\tilde{\nu}$ of $^{1}\text{H}^{35}\text{Cl}$:
$$m_{\text{eff}} = \frac{1.008 \cdot 34.97}{1.008 + 34.97}\text{ u} = 0.9798\text{ u} \cdot 1.66054 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg u}^{-1} = \mathbf{1.627 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}}$$ $$\tilde{\nu} = \frac{1}{2\pi \cdot c}\sqrt{\frac{k_f}{m_{\text{eff}}}} = \frac{1}{2\pi \cdot 2.9979 \times 10^{10}\text{ cm s}^{-1}}\sqrt{\frac{516.0\text{ N m}^{-1}}{1.627 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg}}} = \mathbf{2990\text{ cm}^{-1}}$$
Step (c) – Lambert–Beer Law (`L25` Ex. 7):
$$A_1 = -\log_{10}(0.480) = \mathbf{0.3188}, \qquad \varepsilon = \frac{A_1}{c \cdot L_1} = \frac{0.3188}{0.0100\text{ mol L}^{-1} \cdot 0.200\text{ cm}} = \mathbf{159.4\text{ L mol}^{-1}\text{ cm}^{-1}}$$ $$\text{For } L_2 = 2 L_1 = 0.400\text{ cm}: \quad A_2 = 2 A_1 = 0.6375 \implies T_2 = 10^{-0.6375} = 0.2304 = \mathbf{23.0\%}$$
