1. 90-Min Double Unit Rhythm

• Phase 1 (20 min): Q&A on previous exercises or short 15-min chapter test (Units 3, 6, 9).
• Phase 2 (25 min): Guided Modeling — step-by-step demonstration of 2 key problems.
• Phase 3 (45 min): Active Workshop — start solving your personal HTML worksheet in class; finish remaining steps at home.

2. Continuous Assessment

• 40% Weekly Worksheets: Enter your Student ID, solve the steps (assisted by instant unit-trap hints), and paste your Verification Token into MS Forms.
• 60% Three Short Chapter Tests: 15–20 min digital tests in Units 3, 6, and 9 (20% each).

3. Five "By Heart" Formulae

A formula & constants sheet is provided in all quizzes/exams, except for these 5 basic laws which must be known by heart (no Periodic Table allowed!):

\(\rho = \frac{m}{V}\),  \(n = \frac{m}{M}\),  \(c = \frac{n}{V}\)
\(pV = nRT\),  \(x_i = \frac{n_i}{n_{\text{tot}}}\)
