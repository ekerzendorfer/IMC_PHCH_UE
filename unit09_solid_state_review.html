1. Unit Cells, Miller Indices & Bragg's Law (L26)

Cubic Unit Cell Geometry (Atomic Radius $r$, Lattice Parameter $a$, Multiplicity $Z$):

$$\text{sc } (Z=1): \; a = 2r, \qquad \text{bcc } (Z=2): \; \sqrt{3}\,a = 4r \implies a = \frac{4r}{\sqrt{3}}$$ $$\text{fcc } (Z=4): \; \sqrt{2}\,a = 4r \implies a = \frac{4r}{\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2}\,r$$

Crystal Mass Density $\rho$ & Protein Molar Mass $M$:

$$\rho = \frac{Z \cdot M}{N_A \cdot V_{\text{cell}}} = \frac{Z \cdot M}{N_A \cdot a^3} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad M = \frac{\rho \cdot N_A \cdot a^3}{Z}$$

Interplanar Spacing $d_{hkl}$ & Bragg's Diffraction Law ($n = 1, 2, \dots$):

$$\text{Cubic: } d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2 + k^2 + l^2}}, \qquad \text{Orthorhombic: } \frac{1}{d_{hkl}^2} = \frac{h^2}{a^2} + \frac{k^2}{b^2} + \frac{l^2}{c^2}$$ $$n \cdot \lambda = 2 \cdot d_{hkl} \cdot \sin\theta \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \sin\theta = \frac{n \cdot \lambda}{2 \cdot d_{hkl}}$$

2. NMR Spectroscopy & Molecular Modes (L25)

Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (Larmor Frequency $\nu_L$ & Shielding $\sigma$):

$$\nu_L = \frac{\gamma_N \cdot |B_0|}{2\pi}, \qquad B_{\text{eff}} = B_0(1 - \sigma), \qquad \frac{\nu_{L,2}}{\nu_{L,1}} = \frac{B_{0,2}}{B_{0,1}}$$

NMR Chemical Shift $\delta$ (in $\text{ppm}$) & Spin–Spin Splitting ($M = 2 n_{\text{eq}} I + 1$):

$$\delta\text{ [ppm]} = \frac{\nu_{\text{sample}} - \nu_{\text{ref}}}{\nu_{\text{spec}}} \times 10^6 = \frac{\Delta\nu\text{ [Hz]}}{\nu_{\text{spec}}\text{ [MHz]}}$$ $$\Delta\nu_{12}\text{ [Hz]} = |\delta_1 - \delta_2|\text{ [ppm]} \cdot \nu_{\text{spec}}\text{ [MHz]}$$

Vibrational Normal Modes ($N$ Atoms): Linear: $N_{\text{vib}} = 3N - 5$; Non-linear: $N_{\text{vib}} = 3N - 6$.

📊 Cubic Crystal Systems Quick Reference:
  • Simple Cubic (sc, e.g. $\alpha$-Po): $Z = 1$, Coordination $= 6$, Packing efficiency $= 52.4\%$
  • Body-Centered Cubic (bcc, e.g. Na, K, Fe, W): $Z = 2$, Coordination $= 8$, Packing $= 68.0\%$
  • Face-Centered Cubic (fcc / ccp, e.g. Cu, Ag, Au, Al): $Z = 4$, Coordination $= 12$, Packing $= 74.0\%$
  • Miller Indices $(hkl)$: Take reciprocals of axis intercepts $(p, q, r) \rightarrow (1/p, 1/q, 1/r)$ and multiply by the least common denominator to clear fractions ($-1$ is written $\bar{1}$).
⚠️ Golden Unit Conversion Rules (Crystallography & NMR):
  • Picometers to Centimeters: $1\text{ pm} = 10^{-12}\text{ m} = 10^{-10}\text{ cm} \implies 1\text{ pm}^3 = 10^{-30}\text{ cm}^3$
  • Nanometers to Centimeters: $1\text{ nm} = 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 10^{-7}\text{ cm} \implies 1\text{ nm}^3 = 10^{-21}\text{ cm}^3$
  • Protein Mass: $1\text{ kDa} = 1\text{ kg mol}^{-1} = 1000\text{ g mol}^{-1}$
  • Proton ($^1\text{H}$) Larmor Anchor: $\nu_L(^1\text{H}) = 42.576\text{ MHz}$ at $B_0 = 1.000\text{ T}$ (Lecture quiz value: $42.6\text{ MHz T}^{-1}$)

Guided Example 1 • Ref: L26_Exercises - Solid State.pdf (Exercises 3 & 5)

Bragg Diffraction & Crystal Density of Polonium (Ex. 3) + Protein Molar Mass of Tobacco Seed Globulin (Ex. 5)

Problem Statement:
Part I (`L26` Ex. 3 – Polonium): Polonium ($M = 209.0\text{ g mol}^{-1}$) crystallizes in a primitive cubic lattice ($Z = 1$). First-order ($n = 1$) Bragg diffraction of X-rays with wavelength $\lambda = 150.0\text{ pm}$ from the $\{111\}$ planes occurs at a glancing angle of $\theta = 23.45^\circ$.
Part II (`L26` Ex. 5 – Tobacco Seed Globulin): The protein tobacco seed globulin forms face-centered cubic (fcc, $Z = 4$) crystals with unit cell edge length $a = 12.3\text{ nm}$ and a crystal density of $\rho = 1.287\text{ g cm}^{-3}$ ($N_A = 6.0221 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$).
(a) Calculate the interplanar spacing $d_{111}$ (in $\text{pm}$) and the cubic lattice constant $a$ (in $\text{pm}$ and $\text{cm}$) of polonium.
(b) Calculate the mass density $\rho$ of crystalline polonium (in $\text{g cm}^{-3}$).
(c) Determine the molar mass $M$ of the tobacco seed globulin protein (in $\text{g mol}^{-1}$ and $\text{kDa}$), assuming one protein molecule per fcc lattice point.

🎙️ Lecturer Board Moderation (Guided Example 1):

"Show the two-step bridge from X-ray diffraction to macroscopic density: Bragg's law ($n\lambda = 2 d_{111}\sin\theta$) gives the spacing between diagonal $\{111\}$ planes ($d_{111} = 188.45\text{ pm}$), and cubic geometry ($a = d_{111}\sqrt{1^2+1^2+1^2} = d_{111}\sqrt{3}$) gives the unit cell edge $a = 326.4\text{ pm}$!"

"Warn students about the #1 cubic volume unit trap: since $1\text{ pm} = 10^{-10}\text{ cm}$ and $1\text{ nm} = 10^{-7}\text{ cm}$, it is MUCH safer to convert $a$ into $\text{cm}$ BEFORE cubing ($a^3$) than trying to convert $\text{pm}^3$ or $\text{nm}^3$ afterwards!"

Step (a) – Interplanar Spacing $d_{111}$ & Unit Cell Edge $a$ of Polonium:
$$d_{111} = \frac{\lambda}{2 \cdot \sin\theta} = \frac{150.0\text{ pm}}{2 \cdot \sin(23.45^\circ)} = \frac{150.0\text{ pm}}{2 \cdot 0.39795} = \mathbf{188.47\text{ pm}}$$ $$a = d_{111} \cdot \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2} = 188.47\text{ pm} \cdot \sqrt{3} = \mathbf{326.4\text{ pm}} = \mathbf{3.264 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}}$$
Step (b) – Crystal Density $\rho$ of Primitive Cubic Polonium ($Z = 1$):
$$V_{\text{cell}} = a^3 = (3.2644 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm})^3 = 3.4786 \times 10^{-23}\text{ cm}^3$$ $$\rho = \frac{Z \cdot M}{N_A \cdot a^3} = \frac{1 \cdot 209.0\text{ g mol}^{-1}}{6.0221 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1} \cdot 3.4786 \times 10^{-23}\text{ cm}^3} = \mathbf{9.98\text{ g cm}^{-3}}$$
Step (c) – Molar Mass $M$ of Tobacco Seed Globulin (fcc, $Z = 4$):

Convert $a = 12.3\text{ nm} = 1.23 \times 10^{-6}\text{ cm} \implies V_{\text{cell}} = a^3 = (1.23 \times 10^{-6}\text{ cm})^3 = 1.8609 \times 10^{-18}\text{ cm}^3$:

$$M = \frac{\rho \cdot N_A \cdot a^3}{Z} = \frac{1.287\text{ g cm}^{-3} \cdot 6.0221 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1} \cdot 1.8609 \times 10^{-18}\text{ cm}^3}{4} = \mathbf{3.606 \times 10^5\text{ g mol}^{-1}} = \mathbf{360.6\text{ kDa}}$$
Guided Example 2 • Ref: L26_Exercises - Solid State.pdf (Ex. 6 & 7.2) & T02_Try_Tests_Lecture2.pdf

Orthorhombic Aragonite ($\text{CaCO}_3$) Bragg Diffraction (Ex. 7.2) & High-Field $^1\text{H}$-NMR Spectroscopy

Problem Statement:
Part I (`L26` Ex. 7.2 – Orthorhombic Aragonite): Calcium carbonate in the form of aragonite has an orthorhombic unit cell with lattice parameters $a = 574.1\text{ pm}$, $b = 796.8\text{ pm}$, and $c = 495.9\text{ pm}$.
Part II (`T02` – $^1\text{H}$-NMR & Normal Modes): The Larmor frequency of a "naked" proton ($^1\text{H}$) at $B_0 = 1.00\text{ T}$ is $42.6\text{ MHz}$. On a $600\text{ MHz}$ $^1\text{H}$-NMR spectrometer, the $\text{CH}_2$ protons of ethyl ethanoate ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOCH}_2\text{CH}_3$) appear as a quartet at $\delta = 4.12\text{ ppm}$.
(a) Calculate the interplanar spacing $d_{111}$ (in $\text{pm}$) of orthorhombic aragonite.
(b) Calculate the glancing angle $\theta$ (in $^\circ$) for the $\{111\}$ reflection of aragonite using X-rays of wavelength $\lambda = 83.42\text{ pm}$.
(c) Calculate the magnetic field $B_0$ (in $\text{T}$) of the $600\text{ MHz}$ NMR magnet and the frequency shift $\Delta\nu = \nu_{\text{sample}} - \nu_{\text{TMS}}$ (in $\text{Hz}$) of the $\text{CH}_2$ quartet at $\delta = 4.12\text{ ppm}$.

🎙️ Lecturer Board Moderation (Guided Example 2):

"Contrast cubic vs. orthorhombic crystals on the board: in an orthorhombic crystal ($a \neq b \neq c$, all angles $90^\circ$), we CANNOT pull $a$ out of the square root! We must compute $\frac{1}{d_{hkl}^2} = \frac{h^2}{a^2} + \frac{k^2}{b^2} + \frac{l^2}{c^2}$ term by term and take the inverse square root at the end."

"In Step (c), remind students why chemical shift $\delta$ is defined in $\text{ppm}$ ($\text{Hz}/\text{MHz}$): $\delta = 4.12\text{ ppm}$ is identical on every NMR instrument worldwide, whereas the frequency shift in $\text{Hz}$ ($\Delta\nu = 4.12 \times 600 = 2472\text{ Hz}$) scales linearly with the magnet strength!"

Step (a) – Orthorhombic Spacing $d_{111}$ of Aragonite:
$$\frac{1}{d_{111}^2} = \frac{1^2}{(574.1\text{ pm})^2} + \frac{1^2}{(796.8\text{ pm})^2} + \frac{1^2}{(495.9\text{ pm})^2} = (3.0341 + 1.5751 + 4.0664) \times 10^{-6}\text{ pm}^{-2} = 8.6756 \times 10^{-6}\text{ pm}^{-2}$$ $$d_{111} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{8.6756 \times 10^{-6}\text{ pm}^{-2}}} = \mathbf{339.5\text{ pm}}$$
Step (b) – Bragg Diffraction Angle $\theta$ for $\{111\}$ ($\lambda = 83.42\text{ pm}$):
$$\sin\theta = \frac{\lambda}{2 \cdot d_{111}} = \frac{83.42\text{ pm}}{2 \cdot 339.5\text{ pm}} = 0.12285 \implies \theta = \arcsin(0.12285) = \mathbf{7.06^\circ}$$
Step (c) – $600\text{ MHz}$ NMR Magnetic Induction $B_0$ & Shift $\Delta\nu$:
$$B_0 = \frac{600\text{ MHz}}{42.6\text{ MHz T}^{-1}} = \mathbf{14.08\text{ T}}$$ $$\Delta\nu = \delta\text{ [ppm]} \cdot \nu_{\text{spec}}\text{ [MHz]} = 4.12\text{ ppm} \cdot 600\text{ MHz} = \mathbf{2472\text{ Hz}}$$
